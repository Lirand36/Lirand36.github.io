Forecasting

Signature Path Forecast

Forecast what customers say, not what reps hope.

Replace stage-based probabilities with customer-backed deal evidence. Turn real conversations into transparent buying signals and a forecast leadership can challenge.

Every forecast number traces back to something a customer actually said.

Built withNext.js · React · TypeScript · IndexedDB · Vitest · Playwright · Slack · Vercel

See the reasoning

A forecast the CRO can challenge, line by line.

The problem

Most forecasts multiply deal value by a stage probability. But a $20M deal never lands at $16M. It lands at $20M, or at zero.

Traditional forecasting relies heavily on CRM stages, probability assumptions and rep judgment. The result is a number that looks precise but often says little about what the customer has actually committed to.

What I built

An evidence-based forecasting engine that reads customer emails and call transcripts, extracts proof of commercial progress using AI, and applies deterministic business rules to determine where each deal stands on its path to signature.

Instead of asking what stage a rep selected, it asks what the customer actually said.

Left out, on purpose

Rep overrides that blend evidence with optimism.

No manually adjusted probabilities disguised as customer commitment. The system separates extracted evidence from the rules that determine forecast status.

The result

Every number in the forecast points back to something a customer actually said.

A transparent forecasting model where leadership can inspect the evidence behind a deal, challenge the reasoning and distinguish genuine buying progress from pipeline optimism.

The principle behind itAI reads the mess. Rules handle the money.

Lead routing

Live Event Lead Engine

Turn booth scans into sales-ready conversations.

Transform event badge scans into enriched, qualified leads. AI researches each contact, identifies persona and fit, and routes the lead to the right rep with a relevant next action.

From badge scan to actionable follow-up in seconds.

Built withMake · Gemini · AI web search · HubSpot · Slack · Webhooks

See the reasoning

The conversation shouldn't start when the event ends.

The problem

Every event generates a stream of potential customers. But a badge scan alone tells a sales rep almost nothing about who they just met.

By the time contacts are manually enriched, researched, qualified and assigned, valuable context is lost and promising conversations go cold.

What I built

An automated event lead engine that turns a badge scan into an actionable sales handoff.

The workflow enriches contact and company information, uses AI to research the prospect, identifies persona and qualification tier, and routes the lead to the appropriate sales rep with context for the next conversation.

Left out, on purpose

Treating every badge scan as an equally valuable lead.

Rather than flooding Sales with unqualified contacts, the workflow distinguishes between personas and fit, so reps receive context and prioritization instead of another generic lead notification.

The result

A badge scan becomes a researched, categorized and routed sales opportunity, with a clear next action attached.

The principle behind itSpeed creates value only when context travels with it.

Account planning

Quarterly Expansion Plan from Messy Data

Turn four messy datasets into a Q1 revenue plan.

Reconcile four fragmented CSV exports, uncover expansion opportunities and translate scattered commercial signals into a prioritized Q1 2027 action plan for the CRO.

Four messy exports. Five revenue plays. Clear ownership.

Built withPython · Gemini · Claude Code · HTML

See the reasoning

From fragmented exports to a revenue strategy the CRO can act on.

The problem

The CRO needed an actionable Q1 2027 expansion plan, but the information needed to build one was scattered across four messy CSV exports.

Customer records, commercial information and product usage signals weren't cleanly aligned. There was no single reliable view of which accounts represented genuine expansion opportunities.

The challenge wasn't building another report. It was finding defensible commercial opportunities in fragmented data.

What I built

An analytical workflow that reconciles four independent data sources, resolves account identities, connects commercial and product signals, and evaluates expansion opportunities.

The output translates raw data into five prioritized revenue plays, each with a clear rationale, an owner and a recommended next action.

Left out, on purpose

False precision disguised as a revenue forecast.

Rather than treating incomplete or mismatched records as reliable opportunities, the analysis makes assumptions visible, separates confirmed signals from hypotheses and keeps uncertain account matches out of unsupported conclusions.

The result

A CRO-ready Q1 2027 expansion strategy built from four fragmented data sources.

Five actionable plays that turn scattered customer information into a structured set of revenue opportunities and decisions.

The principle behind itClean the signal before you trust the strategy.

Pipeline health

Deal Intel

Stop guessing which deals deserve attention.

Turn CRM activity into transparent deal-health signals, a 0–100 score and a clear next action, helping reps focus on the opportunities that need attention now.

One deal. One clear signal. One next action.

Built withNext.js · TypeScript · HubSpot API · Vitest · Render

See the reasoning

Give every deal a reason to move, or a reason to worry.

The problem

Sales teams have plenty of CRM data, but very little of it tells reps what they should actually do next.

Activity, engagement and deal signals are scattered across records. Managers struggle to spot which opportunities are progressing, which are stalling and where intervention could make a difference.

What I built

A deal intelligence engine that evaluates six signals from CRM activity, translates them into a transparent 0–100 deal score and surfaces a recommended next action.

Instead of asking reps to interpret another dashboard, the system turns deal signals into a practical daily priority.

Left out, on purpose

Another black-box scoring model.

A score without an explanation creates skepticism, not adoption. The system makes its signals and reasoning visible so reps can understand why a deal needs attention.

The result

A structured view of deal health that connects every score to its underlying signals and a recommended action.

Less time interpreting CRM activity. More time deciding what to do about it.

The principle behind itA score is only useful when someone knows what to do with it.

Revenue operations

Revenue Team Hub

One closed deal. Seven systems in sync.

Turn a closed-won event into coordinated updates across seven business tools, connecting Sales, Support and Customer Success through a single workflow.

One business event triggers the next action across seven tools.

Built withNode.js · JavaScript · HubSpot · Intercom · Jira · Slack · Snowflake · Claude · Google Calendar

See the reasoning

A closed deal shouldn't trigger seven manual processes.

The problem

Closing a deal is the beginning of several operational workflows.

Sales, Support and Customer Success each need updated information, but the handoff between teams often depends on manual updates across disconnected systems.

The result is duplicated work, inconsistent records and unnecessary friction at the moment a customer transitions from prospect to customer.

What I built

A cross-system revenue workflow that uses a closed-won event and business rules to coordinate updates and trigger the appropriate next actions across seven connected tools.

One business event becomes the trigger for a consistent operational process across the revenue organization.

Left out, on purpose

Treating each system as an isolated integration.

The workflow is organized around the business event and its downstream requirements, rather than creating disconnected automations with no shared understanding of the customer lifecycle.

The result

A coordinated closed-won workflow that connects seven tools and reduces manual handoffs between Sales, Support and Customer Success.

The principle behind itAutomate the business process, not just the individual tools.

Data quality

Dedupe Tool

Fix CRM duplicates without sacrificing data integrity.

Identify duplicate records, resolve conflicting field values and preserve the reasoning behind every merge decision.

Every surviving value has a reason.

Built withTypeScript · Next.js · Postgres · Vitest · Render

See the reasoning

Clean CRM data without losing the context behind it.

The problem

Duplicate CRM records create more than administrative noise.

They fragment customer history, compromise reporting, trigger inconsistent automations and make it harder for revenue teams to trust the information they rely on.

But merging records isn't simply about identifying two similar names. Conflicting field values can contain important information that shouldn't disappear during cleanup.

What I built

A deduplication workflow that identifies potential duplicates, evaluates matching confidence against a defined threshold, tags field-level decisions and produces a cleaner consolidated record.

The process uses a high-confidence matching threshold of 0.95 to distinguish reliable matches from records that require more scrutiny.

Left out, on purpose

Blind merging based on name similarity alone.

A duplicate match doesn't automatically mean every field should be overwritten. The process preserves the reasoning behind field-level decisions instead of silently discarding potentially valuable information.

The result

Cleaner CRM records with greater data consistency and traceability.

Every surviving field value has a reason behind it, making the cleanup process easier to trust and audit.

The principle behind itData quality isn't just about removing duplicates. It's about preserving the right information.
